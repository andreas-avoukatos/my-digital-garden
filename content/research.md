I do not have a clear way of thinking what research is supposed to be, (as I do not know what research is), and I found myself to be paralysed from the absence of some form of a defined procedure (that at its end it will grant me a master's thesis / degree); 

I am blessed with being given a clearly defined question (that is open in the literature, and is to be answered by my thesis), but my understanding isn't close enough to begin thinking of an answer.

The (beginners?) approach of "read papers and your understanding will get better the more you do this" seems to contain a lot of faith if you ask me, and I am not used to faith-driven study of any sorts.

At the moment, the purpose of this note is continually dump what I think the "research procedure" is (or should be) to me, with the goal of  
- developing my meta-understanding, by getting a concrete idea of "what I do", (or what I should be doing if what I do isn't helping) - so far I don't have a concrete answer on "what it is that you do", when asked in a social context.
- wasting less time with meta-arguments about the nature of what I should be doing, and actually just do (the so-far-undefined) thing.

As time passes, this note will obtain a clearer form. Let me know if this (eventually) happens to be helpful! :)
