Greece's (mandatory) military service is usually an eye-opening experience for those who go through the trouble. In order to prepare mentally for my enlistment, I asked around for personal accounts. Regarding the training, friends who did their service earlier gave me the impression that it will be a boring experience - mostly just doing the bare minimum required of privates.

To my surprise, my recruit training center did a remarkable job. We had rigorous physical and theoretical training on a daily basis, following the defence minister's "new agenda" for the Greek army to the letter. It was an interesting experience, but admittedly, it left absolutely no time nor energy for studying.

At the end of basic training, everyone is getting assigned a specialty depending either on random chance or prior experience. Being a [[photo.png|musician]], I got transferred to a military band position in early June. Now, I finally have the time and the energy to dive back into the interesting happenings in the math world! :)
