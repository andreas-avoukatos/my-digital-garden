Greece's (mandatory) military service is usually an eye-opening experience among those who went through the trouble. In order to prepare mentally for my enrollment, I asked around for particular experiences. With regards to the training, my friends who did their survey at an earlier time gave me the impression that it will be a boring experience - that is by doing the bare minimum that is typically required - as it was indeed the case for privates.

To my surprise, my newcomers center did a remarkable job with training - both physical and theoretical, both on a daily basis - following the defence minister's "new agenda" for the greek army to the teeth. It was an interesting experience, but  admittedly there was no time nor energy for studying.

At the end of the training, everyone is getting assigned a specialty depending either on random chance or prior experience. Being a musician, I got transferred to a musician's position on early June, and now I have the time and the energy to deal with interesting happenings in the math world :)
