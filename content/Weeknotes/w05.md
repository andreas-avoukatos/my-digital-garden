## $\le$ 01-31 Friday 
As this is my first weeknote, I must start by saying that I've been studying "[SOGATs by example](https://akaposi.github.io/sogat-paris.pdf)" for the last few weeks. At the time of writing this, I am  stuck on exercise 37 which asks for a presyntax (minimal untyped AST) for Simply Typed Combinator Calculus, so that type inference always works. At first I got a strong reaction reading "untyped" (which made me think that I need to figure out a theory devoid of all types, but) I saw that there is a need of having types as information in the AST, while resulting to an untyped model overall. I have to flesh out the details on this...

Nonetheless, I had a bit of fun with CC; I figured out doubling (SII), which lead to an entertaining hunt for the fixed point combinator Y, on the way of figuring out how to do arithmetic in SK.

I have to admit, computation without variables is a fascinating concept (SK combinators are Turing complete).
