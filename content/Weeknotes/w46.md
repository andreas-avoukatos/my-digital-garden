# 1. Understanding $\ne$ solving it
The first concrete task is therefore to extract the question from its paper and write a document of perhaps two pages explaining:
- The exact mathematical statement being investigated.
- The ambient type theory and assumptions under which it is formulated.
- The definitions necessary even to state it.
- The existing results that appear closest to answering it.
- The specific obstruction that prevents those results from immediately settling the question

# 2. Learn prerequisites backwards from the problem
construct a dependency graph of the concepts you actually need rather than "read a substantial category theory textbook, then study categorical logic, then study the general theory of higher inductive types, and finally return to the original paper."
