My name is Andreas Avoukatos and I am currently a master's student at the [Algorithms, Logic and Discrete Mathematics](http://alma.di.uoa.gr) programme, University of Athens, Greece. 

It is my belief that all mathematicians should use proof verification software (as checking a proof is usually an arduous process), but in order to do so one needs to learn a functional programming language aside, at the moment. I was happy to hear (and attend a course) about Homotopy Type Theory, and currently I am expanding my knowledge in order to come up with a suitable master's thesis topic. 

In the meantime, I plan to use this corner of the internet as a collection of all the cool things I learn or come up with - something like a study diary, if you wish - and for completeness (wrt my introduction), you can find my CV [here](http://users.uoa.gr/~aavoukatos/Avoukatos_cv.pdf).
