# Greetings! 

My name is Andreas Avoukatos, and I am a master's student in the [Algorithms, Logic and Discrete Mathematics](http://alma.di.uoa.gr) programme at the Department of Informatics, National and Kapodistrian University of Athens, Greece.

My main interest is the mathematics behind proof assistants, as my main gripe during my undergraduate studies was the fact that computers weren't as useful as they were to the applied mathematician, (at least in my department, having experienced a bit of both worlds). I am glad I came across Homotopy Type Theory in my graduate studies, and the fact that I was able to attend to a couple of workshops in the broader field abroad! :)

# What's this?
The main goal is to use this corner of the internet as a study diary, as I'm going through my thesis. On the left you can find my [[Weeknotes/|weekly notes]], and a few words about some [[Masterclasses - workshops/|events]] I've had the opportunity to attend. 

This website is (and will be) a work-in-progress. Blogs have each post to be complete at the time of publishing, and most usually the chronological order is of some importance. When it comes to taking notes there's probably continuous growth and interconnectedness, starting from a seedling (barely started notes, more thought is needed), that grow to become a sapling (good amount of content but work needs to be done), and ending up like a tree (mostly done pieces of thought, shouldn't change much apart from editorial touches); of course there can be other organisational concepts, but that's the gist of a digital garden.

(In case you're interested in RSS feeds, here's mine! [RSS feed](https://andreas-avoukatos.github.io/my-digital-garden/index.xml))
